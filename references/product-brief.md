# Product brief for a new user

Ask for the **required** fields before researching final topics. The user can answer in plain text; this is a guide, not a form they must complete verbatim. Store their answers with the run, outside this skill's repository.

## Required

1. **Product or service:** name, a plain-language description, and a website if one exists.
2. **Capabilities and limits:** what it actually does, major features or offers, what it cannot do, and claims that must not be made.
3. **Readers:** target personas, jobs or problems, and buying or usage stage.
4. **Market and language:** target geography, language, and any regional restrictions.
5. **Blog goal:** the action or outcome the content should support, such as education, qualified traffic, leads, or product adoption.

If any required answer is missing, ask for that answer. A website or competitor page may verify details, but the user must provide their own product description and audience. If the user's supplied claim conflicts with the public product page, flag it before using the claim in a final brief.

## Useful when available

- Existing blog URL, sitemap, topic tracker, planned or draft topics, and content that must not be duplicated.
- Preferred themes or categories; excluded topics; editorial voice and citation rules.
- Direct competitors or alternatives, with a note about comparison boundaries.
- Priority features, pages, conversion destinations, or customer questions.
- Search Console, site-search, keyword-platform, support, or sales-question data the user authorizes for this task.
- Number of topics, publishing capacity, relevant dates, and other campaigns that share the calendar.
- Output format and destination: chat, Markdown, CSV, spreadsheet, or a connected topic tracker.
- Approval or review owners, especially for legal, medical, financial, or other regulated claims.

## First-message prompt to adapt

> To plan topics for your product, please tell me what it does and cannot do, who you want to reach, the target country/language, and the blog's goal. Share your website and existing topic list if you have them. I can use your preferred categories and publishing capacity, or propose both after reading your brief.

Do not preload another company's profile into this prompt. Do not put user-specific answers back into `SKILL.md` or this reference.
